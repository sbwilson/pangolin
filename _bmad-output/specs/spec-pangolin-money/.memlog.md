topic: Pangolin Money — self-hosted household finance app: architecture and data model
updated: 2026-10-05T20:06

- (capability) CAP-1 Multi-format bank/broker import: household can import transactions from CommBank (OFX/CSV/QIF), ubank (CSV), Up (CSV history), any bank/broker PDF statement (LLM-extracted, checked against statement balances), CMC Invest (CSV) and Betashares/super (units x price) through one idempotent pipeline; success = each format ships a committed anonymised sample, re-import of the same file or overlapping range changes nothing, and any statement with a printed balance reconciles to the cent.
- (capability) CAP-2 Transaction classification: split is categorised deterministically by rule, then payee default, then LLM suggestion, with LLM auto-apply gated by a tuned confidence threshold; success = every auto-applied split records model/prompt-version/outcome and everything else lands in the review inbox.
- (capability) CAP-3 Two-person privacy model: an account can be marked private (owner-only) and a shared-account transaction's name hidden from the partner for up to 12 months, while shared totals stay correct; success = cross-user reads of private accounts or hidden names fail in tests, hidden names lift automatically after 12 months, and redaction runs through one visibleAccounts()/redact() path.
- (capability) CAP-4 Budgets: household or person sets a per-category/group cap on a fortnight or month cycle anchored to a payday, with spent/pace/projected computed live from splits; success = spent/pace/projected match a hand-computed check against seeded splits and rollover behaves per its per-budget setting.
- (capability) CAP-5 Recurring bill detection: the app detects recurring bills from payee/interval/amount patterns and alerts on a missed payment or a >10% price rise; success = a synthetic series of >=3 occurrences with a stable interval is detected and one-click confirmed, and a seeded 11%+ rise raises an alert.
- (capability) CAP-6 Goals and staged savings allocation: positive new savings each period are split across goals by staged, priority-ordered rules, with automatic stage transitions and rescaling on goal completion or fallback below threshold; success = a seeded multi-period savings history produces goal_allocation rows matching the active stage's shares, and a completed or under-threshold goal triggers the documented rescale/reactivation with a logged stage change.
- (capability) CAP-7 Savings reconciliation invariant: each period and after every import, goal balances + unallocated buffer must equal the real balance of that pool's savings accounts, flagging over-commitment or mismatch; success = a seeded over-commitment (buffer exhausted) shows a red shortfall on the goals page and review inbox, and any other mismatch offers a one-click buffer adjustment.
- (capability) CAP-8 Forecasting: cash flow (3-12 months per account, low-balance warning) and net worth (assets/liabilities with editable savings-rate/return/mortgage/super assumptions) are projected from known pay cycles, confirmed bills and budgeted spend; success = a seeded pay-cycle + bills + budget scenario produces a cash-flow projection whose low-balance warning fires at the expected date.
- (capability) CAP-9 Investment lot accounting: ETF holdings (Betashares, CMC) are tracked as buy/sell/distribution/DRP/cost-base-adjustment events, with FIFO or manually-selected lots, 12-month CGT discount flagging, and both XIRR and time-weighted return; success = a seeded buy/sell/DRP/adjustment sequence produces lot-level cost bases and a capital gain on sale matching a hand-computed figure, with the 50% discount applied only to lots held over 12 months.
- (capability) CAP-10 Super tracking: super balance is derived as units x daily unit price per option, contributions are tagged by kind and tracked against per-FY concessional caps (with carry-forward), and expected SG per payday is checked against contributions received; success = a seeded statement + unit-price history reproduces the fund's reported balance within rounding, the cap tracker flags a seeded over-cap contribution, and a seeded missed SG payment is flagged after its payday.
- (capability) CAP-11 Investment property tracking: a property record links its loan account, rental income and costs to show net cash position per month/FY and gearing (loan / valuation), with repayments treated as a simple expense; success = a seeded property's rent/repayments/costs produce the documented net cash position and gearing figure, visible in the owner's view and hidden from the partner if the loan account is private.
- (capability) CAP-12 Tax pack generation: produces a per-person, per-FY pack of deductions by ATO label, investment income and capital gains, and super contributions vs caps, exportable as CSV and a PDF bundle with receipts; success = a seeded FY of deductible splits, distributions and lot sales produces a deduction summary, capital gains schedule and cap comparison matching hand-computed totals, exported in both formats.
- (capability) CAP-13 Activities: a split can carry an activity tag (e.g. Japan Trip 2026) with dates and budget, rolling up total/by-category/by-person/against-budget, and pre-filling suggestions for transactions in its date range; success = a seeded dated activity shows the correct rollups and offers itself as a suggestion for a seeded transaction inside its date range.
- (capability) CAP-14 Shared spending and contribution: every split has a beneficiary (shared or a person), and each person's contribution to shared costs (transfers into shared accounts + shared expenses paid personally) drives an always-visible apportioned share of reports; success = a seeded pair of contribution histories produces the documented apportioned percentages, configurable to 50/50, and this is groundwork for v1.1 partner settlement.
- (capability) CAP-15 Authentication and account recovery: partners authenticate with passkeys (password+TOTP fallback), recover access via one-time recovery codes or partner-assisted re-enrolment, with a server-console reset for both-locked-out; success = tests cover login, passkey/TOTP fallback, recovery-code enrolment-forcing, and partner-assisted reset within its 24-hour expiry and audit log, without leaking the other partner's private data.
- (capability) CAP-16 Self-hosted install, upgrade and disaster recovery: install.sh brings up the full stack in one command on Debian/Ubuntu/Rocky behind an existing reverse proxy (or bundled Caddy/Tailscale-only), and pangolin upgrade/backup/restore keep it running with a tested restore path; success = CI backs up and restores a synthetic database on every release, a monthly restore drill runs automatically on the server, and pangolin upgrade rolls back automatically on a failed health check.
- (constraint) Private data never leaves the server by default; outbound network is limited to an explicit allowlist (price/unit-price hosts, any configured LLM endpoint) -- no third-party aggregators, no telemetry. Rules out any always-on cloud dependency.
- (constraint) SQLite is the single source of truth with a single writer (one Node process/container); rules out a separate queue server (jobs are polled rows in SQLite) and multi-writer scaling designs.
- (constraint) Money is stored as integer minor units (branded Cents type); no floats near money. Units are integer micro-units; prices are decimal strings. Rules out float-based money arithmetic anywhere in the codebase.
- (constraint) Cloud LLM use is opt-in only, assigned per purpose, and receives only description/amount/date/category list -- never account names, people, or private transactions. PDF statement extraction (contains names/addresses/account numbers) is restricted to local providers unless explicitly enabled for cloud. Rules out sending full transaction context to any cloud model by default.
- (constraint) One-command install and upgrade on Debian first (then Ubuntu, Rocky Linux), must work behind an existing reverse proxy (Nginx Proxy Manager), with encrypted backups and a CI-tested restore. Rules out designs needing manual multi-step server configuration or an unproven restore path.
- (constraint) Web and mobile ship as one responsive PWA; rules out building or maintaining a separate native app-store app.
- (constraint) Database file is protected by host disk encryption, not SQLCipher -- explicit decision to avoid a second key to manage and keep standard SQLite tooling working. Rules out application-level DB encryption in v1.
- (constraint) Australian defaults are baked in: AUD base currency, July-June financial year, fortnightly pay cycles, ATO tax categories, super. Rules out currency- or locale-naive date/period/tax logic (ledger stays currency-agnostic but v1 requires account currency = base currency).
- (constraint) STRICT SQLite tables with PRAGMA foreign_keys = ON; every write goes through a service layer that also writes audit_log. Rules out direct/ad-hoc writes bypassing the service layer or audit trail.
- (constraint) Import pipeline must be idempotent: re-importing the same file or an overlapping date range changes nothing. Rules out import designs without stable dedup keys (external_id or account+date+amount+description+occurrence fingerprint).
- (non-goal) Unofficial CommBank/ubank API clients are out of scope (they require storing full-access credentials).
- (non-goal) Scraping member portals (super funds, brokers) is out of scope -- MFA, breakage, credential storage risk.
- (non-goal) Paid third-party account aggregators / Consumer Data Right integrations are out of scope for v1.
- (non-goal) The Up Bank API is out of scope (account is closing); only a one-off CSV history import is supported.
- (non-goal) Full double-entry accounting is a non-goal; the ledger uses transactions+splits with transfer links and lot-level accounting for investments instead.
- (non-goal) Partner settlement (who owes whom for shared costs paid from personal accounts) is deferred to v1.1, after M4.
- (non-goal) Betashares Direct statement parsing is deferred; v1 uses manual holdings entry only.
- (non-goal) Splitting mortgage repayments into interest/principal is deferred; v1 treats repayments as a simple expense.
- (non-goal) Monte Carlo forecasting bands are deferred; v1 ships editable explicit assumptions only.
- (non-goal) A native app-store mobile app is a non-goal; PWA only.
- (decision) Data model shape: one bank line = one transaction with >=1 splits; categories/tax/activity/person all live on the split, so every report sums splits. Transfers are two transactions linked by a transfer_group. Investments/super use unit-based tables, not cash rows.
- (decision) Overspending periods: Option A chosen -- a deficit period draws down the unallocated buffer only; goal balances never decrease from overspending. (Options B pro-rata and C carry-forward were considered and rejected: B is noisy on lumpy fortnights, C pauses allocation until repaid.)
- (decision) Savings definition: balance of accounts flagged as savings (personal + shared); investments (brokerage, super) are tracked separately and never count as savings.
- (decision) Shared spending apportionment: beneficiary lives on the split; each person's contribution to shared costs = transfers into shared accounts + shared expenses paid personally; reports apportion by contribution share (50/50 is a configurable default).
- (decision) ASX price sourcing: Yahoo Finance chart endpoint is primary (unofficial, ticker-only leaves the server), issuer published NAV is fallback, manual override always available; source recorded on each price row.
- (decision) History import starts from 1 July 2024 (FY2025); the on-hand CommBank exports start FY2026 (home loan from March 2025), so earlier history must be exported from NetBank before M1's gate.
- (decision) Hosting: Debian VM on Proxmox with LUKS data-disk encryption unlocked via Clevis+Tang (network-bound, so a stolen disk/backup stays locked); Ubuntu 24.04 and Rocky Linux 9 also supported. Exposure is public via existing Nginx Proxy Manager (Let's Encrypt); Tailscale-only remains a supported stricter option.
- (decision) Backups: restic to a REST server on TrueNAS over WireGuard, append-only (host can add but never delete backups); retention 7 daily/4 weekly/12 monthly; restic check weekly; restore verified via PRAGMA integrity_check, row counts and per-account balance sums against the backup manifest; tested in CI on every release and monthly on the live server.
- (decision) Milestones: five milestones ending in verifiable checks, security/backups/CI first. M1 (import, dedup, transfer matching) is called out as highest-risk since every later report depends on it, so it gets disproportionate time before later milestones proceed.
