Carissa — pick a favourite (or a few to keep as switchable themes)

Six moods for the same screen, each in light and dark. The numbers and layout don’t change, only the colours. Every text colour passes the accessibility contrast check in both modes, so any of these can ship.

  1. Clay & Linen — warm and earthy
  2. Harbour Morning — cool and crisp
  3. Fern Gully — botanical and alive
  4. Galah Party — bold and saturated
  5. Gelato Bar — soft and sweet but grown-up
  6. Black Opal — moody and jewel-toned
