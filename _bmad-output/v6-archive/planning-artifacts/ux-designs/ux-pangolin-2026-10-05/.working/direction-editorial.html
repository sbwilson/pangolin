Bold, editorial, one big number at a time

Treats each screen like a magazine spread: the headline is a sentence about your money, the numbers are set huge in a serif, and rules and whitespace replace cards and chrome. It suits a couple who sit down every month or two and want the story first, detail second; motion is slow and deliberate (numbers count up, flows fade in like ink). Colours are placeholder only — paper, ink and one accent reserved for the number that matters — until Carissa picks the palette.
