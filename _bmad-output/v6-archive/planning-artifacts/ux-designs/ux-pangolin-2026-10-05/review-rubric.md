# Spine Pair Review — Pangolin Money

## Overall verdict
The visual contract is close to source-extractable: every `{token}` reference resolves (66 in DESIGN.md, 2 in EXPERIENCE.md), every colour has a hex value with a light/dark pair across six themes, all the contrast figures I recomputed match, and every `.working/` and `imports/` link resolves. The behavioural contract is lopsided. Cash flow, import, Needs review, Home buying and Loan detail are specified in depth. Budgets (named a priority feature), Goals, Forecast, Settings and Sign-in have only an IA row and an empty state. Three privacy and architecture commitments (partner pending count, in-app restore, ownership split) are either contradicted or missing. Story-dev can start on the Cash flow, ledger and planner epics now. It can't start on Planning (budgets, goals, forecast) or Settings without another pass.

## 1. Flow coverage — thin
Checked: the CAP-1…CAP-18 names from `SPEC.md` (the spec has no UJs; capabilities are the requirement names), plus the journeys the memlog commits to, against `## Key Flows`. Flows 1–4 each have a named protagonist, numbered steps, a bold climax and a failure path. Covered: CAP-1, CAP-2, CAP-3 (Flow 4), CAP-17, plus the spec catch-up surfaces (planner, loan detail, saved plans). CAP-9, 10, 12 and 13 are deferred to M4 (EXPERIENCE § IA, "Later (M4)"), which is defensible.
### Findings
- **critical** Budgeting (CAP-4) was named a priority feature ("Priority features now: home loan planning, spending analysis, budgeting", .memlog.md). It has no flow beyond one sentence (Flow 1 step 6, "Each sets their own budgets and goals"), no component in either spine, and no non-empty state. Spent / pace / projected, the payday-anchored fortnight/month cycle, rollover, per-person vs shared caps and AI-suggested amounts have no presentation. Story-dev would have to invent the whole surface. (EXPERIENCE § Key Flows; § IA row "Budgets"). *Fix:* add a "Set and track a budget" flow (protagonist, steps, climax at the pace/projected read-out, failure path for going over budget), plus a Budget card/row in both component tables.
- **high** Goals with staged allocation and reconciliation (CAP-6, CAP-7) have no flow. The spec's required "red shortfall on the goals page and review inbox" and the "one-click buffer adjustment" are absent, and DESIGN § Colors forbids red except for amounts, so the shortfall colour is undecided. (EXPERIENCE § IA "Goals"; DESIGN § Colors). *Fix:* add a goals flow (allocation after an import → stage transition → over-commitment shortfall → buffer fix) and choose the shortfall token.
- **high** Forecast (CAP-8) has no flow, and nothing covers the low-balance warning, which is the capability's success signal. The only treatment is an empty state. (EXPERIENCE § IA "Forecast"; § State Patterns "Empty / not enough data"). *Fix:* add a forecast flow ending on the low-balance warning date, and specify the projection chart, the assumption editing and its table equivalent.
- **high** CAP-14's "always-visible apportioned share of reports" isn't mentioned anywhere in either spine. The Cash flow grid (DESIGN § Layout & Spacing) has no slot for it. (both spines). *Fix:* decide where the apportioned share sits on Cash flow and Spending, and add it to the layout and to KPI tile or header patterns.
- **medium** Sign-in, recovery and partner-assisted reset (CAP-15) have states and a re-auth dialog but no flow. Recovery-code sign-in that forces a new passkey, and the 24-hour partner reset link, are never walked. (EXPERIENCE § IA rows Sign in / Recover; § Privacy "Partner-assisted reset"). *Fix:* add a short recovery flow with its failure paths (expired link, used code).
- **medium** Recurring bills (CAP-5: detect, one-click confirm, missed or +10% alert) appear only as an "alert" Needs review item kind. (EXPERIENCE § Component Patterns "Needs review item"). *Fix:* specify the confirm-a-detected-bill item and the bill-alert item copy and actions.
- **medium** Property (CAP-11: net cash position per month and FY, gearing) has no surface beyond "properties (incl. investment)" in the Accounts row. The planner's Property card is a different thing. (EXPERIENCE § IA "Accounts"). *Fix:* add a property detail treatment, or state that it lives in Loan detail.
- **low** Flow 1's preamble says "steps 7–10 on one laptop together", but step 11 (Save plan) is also on that laptop. (EXPERIENCE Flow 1). *Fix:* "7–11".

## 2. Token completeness — adequate
Checked: all 73 colour keys (36 light/dark pairs, plus `mascot-ink`, which is fixed by design), 13 typography roles, 5 radii, 15 spacing tokens and 22 component token groups. Every `{path}` reference resolves. I recomputed WCAG ratios from the theme tables, and the stated table figures match (for example Fern light ring 3.19 vs the stated 3.2).
### Findings
- **medium** Only Clay & Linen is in the YAML frontmatter. The other five themes, and Clay's own chart ramp, exist only as markdown tables, so a token generator reading the frontmatter gets one theme of six. (DESIGN § Theme sets). *Fix:* either commit the five themes as frontmatter tokens (for example `harbour-morning.primary`) or state explicitly that the tables are the machine source and give their parse shape.
- **medium** The per-theme derivation rule leaves out `destructive-foreground`. Clay defines it (`#FFFFFF` light, `#1C1713` dark); the other five themes don't. Several shadcn semantic tokens that variants consume are also never defined: `secondary`/`secondary-foreground` (Badge, Button `secondary`) and `sidebar-foreground`/`sidebar-border`/`sidebar-accent`/`sidebar-primary`. "Unlisted shadcn tokens inherit defaults" would pull neutral greys into warm themes. (DESIGN line 359, line 283). *Fix:* extend the derivation rule, for example `destructive-foreground = primary-foreground`, `secondary = background`, `sidebar-* = foreground/border/accent/primary`.
- **low** Contrast isn't stated for the load-bearing pairs used by `nav-badge`, `warning-line`, `nav-item-active` and `hidden-name-row`: warning on warning-bg, and primary on accent. All 12 theme-modes pass when recomputed (minimum 4.72 and 4.57), but the spine doesn't commit to it. (DESIGN § Colors contrast table). *Fix:* add two columns to the contrast table.
- **low** Several pixel values have no token: logo "16px/650" (weight 650 isn't in the type ramp), the "50px" date column, the "10px" stress track and the segmented-control shadow literal. (DESIGN § Components, § Elevation). *Fix:* tokenise them or tag them as fixed.

## 3. Component coverage — thin
Checked: every component named in DESIGN § Components (35 rows plus 19 shadcn as-is) against EXPERIENCE § Component Patterns (28 rows), and every component named in the IA, states and flows.
### Findings
- **high** Components needed by in-scope surfaces are missing from both spines: Budget card/row (spent, pace, projected), Goal card (stage, allocation, shortfall), Forecast chart and assumption editor, Account list row, Transactions filter chips and search, Spending category bars and "flexible categories", Settings cards (Sign-in & security, Recovery codes, Partner, LLM providers, System status), the sign-in form, and the Demo and Offline banners. (DESIGN § Components; EXPERIENCE § Component Patterns). *Fix:* add a visual row and a behavioural row for each, or explicitly mark ones as shadcn as-is with their behaviour.
- **medium** Component names drift between the files: "Sankey card" vs "Sankey"; "Transaction sheet & split editor" vs "Split editor" (the sheet itself has no behavioural row); "Planner result" + "Rate stress bars" vs "Planner result / stress table"; "Rate pill" + "Rate / offset / extra-repayment slider" vs "Rate pills / slider"; "Saved plan row" vs "Saved plan"; "Loan detail" vs "Loan detail cards". (both § Components). *Fix:* use one canonical name per component in both tables.
- **medium** Some rows defer elsewhere instead of giving a rule. "Category chip" is just `{components.category-chip}`, a one-word visual spec with no states (editable, hover). "Import popover" and "Loan detail cards" in Component Patterns say "See …" with no rules of their own. (DESIGN line 459; EXPERIENCE lines 100, 109). *Fix:* give category chip its idle/editable/selected appearance and keep the cross-references, but name the rules they point to.
- **low** Toast has behavioural rules but only "Toast (Sonner)" as-is visually. Stat tile and Person dot have no behavioural row. All three are acceptable as presentational, but say so. (EXPERIENCE line 112; DESIGN line 444).
- **low** Frontmatter component tokens `button-secondary`, `card`, `amount-in` and `amount-out` are never referenced in prose. (DESIGN frontmatter). *Fix:* reference them from the matching component rows.

## 4. State coverage — thin
Checked each IA surface for empty, cold-load, error, no-results, permission/not-found, offline and domain states.
### Findings
- **high** Budgets, Goals and Forecast have only empty states. Over budget, ahead/behind pace, goal complete with stage change, goal shortfall, and the forecast low-balance warning are all missing, and those are the states the spec's success signals test. (EXPERIENCE § State Patterns rows 121, 124). *Fix:* add the domain states, together with the components from finding 3.1.
- **medium** The live planner recalculation has no pending state. Outputs come from the server on every slider move ("debounced server recalculation"), but nothing says what the hero shows while a request is in flight or if it fails. (EXPERIENCE § Interaction Primitives "Sliders"). *Fix:* specify the stale/pending treatment (for example dimmed figure plus a live-region update on settle) and the error fallback.
- **medium** Transactions has no state for a filter or search with no results, although URL-held filters are CAP-18's core. Cash flow and Spending have no state for a range with zero rows when imports do exist. (EXPERIENCE § State Patterns). *Fix:* add "no matches" (with clear filters) and "nothing in this range" states.
- **medium** Re-importing the same file or an overlapping range is idempotent by spec (CAP-1), but the popover's result for "0 new" isn't specified, and neither is an unsupported file type. (EXPERIENCE § Interaction Primitives "Import popover"). *Fix:* add both outcomes with copy.
- **medium** Settings › System status has no states for a failed backup, a failed restore drill or dead jobs (AD-9 says "kind and time only"). /recover and /setup have no error states (used or invalid code, expired partner link). (EXPERIENCE § IA "Settings", rows Setup/Recover). *Fix:* add them, in plain no-cheek copy.

The spines are mostly lean. The large theme and contrast tables carry the code contract, so they aren't bloat.
### Findings
- **low** The Home buying glimpse is described in three places (DESIGN Components, EXPERIENCE Component Patterns, EXPERIENCE § Home Buying Planner "Glimpse on Cash flow"). *Fix:* keep the visual spec in DESIGN and the behaviour in Component Patterns, and drop the third.
- **low** "Spec Catch-up List" is a process hand-off for `bmad-correct-course`, not something a downstream consumer reads. (EXPERIENCE § Spec Catch-up List). *Fix:* move it to the memlog or a separate hand-off note.
- **low** The mock example figures don't agree with each other ("$7,596 a month left" in Accessibility Floor; "~$3,764 spare" in Outputs; "$Y a fortnight" in the climax), which invites readers to treat them as specs. *Fix:* use one consistent worked example or mark them as illustrative.

## 7. Inheritance discipline — thin
Checked: all `sources` resolve (7 in DESIGN, 9 in EXPERIENCE); CAP and AD citations (AD-4, AD-5, AD-9, AD-17, AD-22, `ReauthRequired`, `import_batch.status`, 5-minute window) match the architecture spine; memlog decisions against the spines.
### Findings
- **high** The partner pending chip ("Carissa: 12 left") counts all of the partner's review items. Items on her private accounts would change the count, which contradicts the spine's own Private rule ("no placeholder, no count") and AD-5/AD-22. The spine commits the behaviour while leaving the scope as Open Question 5. (EXPERIENCE § Component Patterns "Partner pending chip"; § State Patterns "Private"; Open Q5). *Fix:* commit to counting shared/public items only (AD-22 most-restrictive scope), or drop the chip.
- **high** Memlog decision "Ownership split on loans/properties must be enterable (Simon currently owns/pays 100%)" isn't in either spine. The Property card and the planner's equity/sell maths don't take ownership share into account. (.memlog.md last entry; DESIGN "Property card"; EXPERIENCE § Home Buying Planner). *Fix:* add an ownership-share input to the Property card and loan setup, and say how it feeds usable equity and rent.
- **high** CAP-17's "current net worth" has no surface (Open Question 6). An in-scope capability has nowhere to go in the IA. (EXPERIENCE § IA; Open Q6). *Fix:* decide the surface, for example a Net worth card on Cash flow or a section in Forecast.
- **medium** Re-auth lists "restore" (and Settings has "restore") as an in-app action. AD-16 makes restore a CLI-only operation on a stopped stack. (EXPERIENCE lines 57, 167). *Fix:* make restore read-only in Settings (drill status, last backup) and remove it from the re-auth triggers. Also reconcile "recovery codes", which isn't in the architecture's re-auth list.
- **medium** Loan detail's "Where your repayments went" designs a card that contradicts the spec non-goal on interest/principal splitting. Open Q1 flags it but it isn't decided. (EXPERIENCE § Loan Detail). *Fix:* decide before stories are cut; mark the card as conditional.
- **medium** Saved plan scope (Open Q4) and the one-laptop planning view ("planning uses only what that person can see") leave data visibility for plans uncommitted, and that shapes the data model. (EXPERIENCE § Privacy; Open Q4). *Fix:* commit to "personal plan, owner's view" or "shared plan, shared inputs only".
- **medium** The two reconcile files list 27 unaddressed ideas surfaced for keep/drop, and the memlog records no decisions on them. Two are real gaps: where Sign out lives, and how uncategorised money appears in the Sankey/P&L. (reconcile-sharkfin.md §3, reconcile-smartspend.md §3). *Fix:* record keep/drop decisions, and specify sign-out and the uncategorised node.
- **low** There's no glossary. Spine terms ("Needs review", "Kept", "Transactions") rename source terms ("review inbox", saved, "ledger") without a mapping. *Fix:* add a short term map in Foundation.

## 8. Shape fit — strong

EXPERIENCE.md has all eight defaults in order, plus Responsive & Platform and Inspiration & Anti-patterns (both triggered). The invented sections (Home Buying Planner, Loan Detail, Privacy & Partner Awareness, Theming) cover net-new or cross-cutting behaviour and earn their place. Open Questions is load-bearing. Spec Catch-up List is addressed under §6.
